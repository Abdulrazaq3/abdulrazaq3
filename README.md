### 👨‍💻 About Me
I am an **AI Engineer** focused on building and deploying end-to-end AI systems, with a specialized focus on **Arabic Large Language Models (LLMs)** and **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**.

- 🔬 Currently working as an **AI Engineer at Saai - AI Lab**, fine-tuning Arabic LLMs (`SFT`, `DPO`), benchmarking model outputs, and serving production inference via `vLLM`.
- ⚙️ Strong foundation in **Backend Engineering** (`Python`, `FastAPI`, `PostgreSQL`), designing scalable APIs and full-stack AI workflows.
- 🔍 Experienced in building **Hybrid RAG pipelines** combining dense vector embeddings (`BGE-M3`, `pgvector`, `ChromaDB`) with lexical search (`BM25`), instrumented with `Phoenix` and `OpenTelemetry`.
